(01)About

I build software that pays attention. It hears the chat spike in a four-hour stream, sees a bank card held to a chest and turns it into centimetres, and counts every kobo so a loan never risks money it didn't earn. Laravel taught me structure. AI gave me leverage. Solana taught me that every byte pays rent.

I'm Paulji Peters, a full-stack engineer from Nigeria with a computer engineering degree and a habit of founding things. I work across the whole stack: Postgres schemas, Laravel and FastAPI backends, Python GPU workers, Next.js frontends and Solana programs.

I like problems where being wrong is expensive: money, measurements, other people's time. So I write the threat model, keep money in integers and build systems that refuse to answer when they aren't sure.
